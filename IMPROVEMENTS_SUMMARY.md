## 🔧 How It Works (Automatic)

Every time bot generates a reel:

1. **Trends.py** → Scrapes 4 sources for hottest topics
2. **Story.py** → AI uses viral hook templates
3. **Hashtags.py** → Generates 15 smart hashtags
4. **Captions.py** → Creates optimized caption with CTAs
5. **Main.py** → Combines everything into viral post
6. **Analytics.py** → Tracks performance for next time

**No configuration needed - it just works!**
